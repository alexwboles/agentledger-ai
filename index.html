Risk register

One-click governance: sanctioned means approved, review means decide soon, blocked means pull its access. Changing a status here only updates the record — you still revoke the real credential yourself.

Shadow check

Discovery scans five local sources and compares them against your registered agents. Anything found but not registered is a shadow agent — the ones that skip every control you set up.

AgentLedger is a self-hosted documentation and scoring tool. It cannot see your real credentials, enforce blocks, or revoke access — every "recommended" action is a suggestion for you to carry out. Data stays in this browser (localStorage); nothing leaves your machine.
